# quiet_shen_fire（10 秒，实际 10.125 秒）

## 简报

写实电影感，雨夜古代纸扎铺，一镜到底的安静戏。三十多岁的沈娘子（低发髻插木簪、浅灰绿麻布上衣、灰蓝长裙，朴素）蹲在铁火盆前守火，往火里添一张黄纸。火光映着她半张脸，门外是冷蓝的雨。画外忽然传来一声纸片窸窣，她手停住，眼睛先往声音方向瞟，头慢半拍才转过去一点；什么也没有，她松了口气，但头没有完全转回来，手里那张黄纸还捏着。镜头从中景慢慢推到她的脸和火光。没有台词。

## Shot 1

**官方：**

[Shot 1] Cinematic, medium shot, the camera slowly pushes in toward a dimly lit, weathered paper craft shop at night. The room is cluttered with hanging paper lanterns and skeletal bamboo frames, illuminated primarily by the orange, flickering glow of a cast-iron brazier in the lower foreground and the cold, desaturated blue light filtering through the rain-streaked wooden shop door. Shen Niangzi (S1), a woman in her thirties with a simple low hair bun secured by a single polished wood hairpin, wearing a muted light-grey-green linen top and a dusty grey-blue skirt, is crouched on the uneven floorboards before the brazier. Her face is partially bathed in warm embers-light, contrasting with the dark, moody shadows of the shop. She carefully feeds a single sheet of yellow ritual paper into the flames, her expression focused and solemn. Suddenly, a distinct sound of dry paper rustling emanates from the dark corner behind her, prompting the woman (S1) to freeze mid-motion; her eyes shift sharply toward the source of the noise before her head turns slowly and cautiously to inspect the shadows, her grip tightening on the remaining yellow paper, before she relaxes slightly but maintains her vigilance, head still angled toward the void as the camera closes in on the flickering flames and her contemplative profile.

**skill：**

[Shot 1] Photoreal cinematic, 16:9. A vast dark hall of an ancient Chinese paper-effigy shop on a rainy night: black wooden pillars, wet stone floor, white paper lotus lanterns hanging from the beams, white paper horses standing in the shadows. Warm orange light from an iron fire brazier at the back left, cold blue rain light from the open doorway at the back. Film grain. A single continuous take in real time. The shot opens as a medium shot of a real woman in her thirties — black hair in a low bun with a wooden hairpin, pale grey-green linen jacket, grey-blue long skirt, plain and unadorned — crouching in front of the iron brazier on the left of the frame, the open doorway and the blue rain behind her on the right. She is the only person in the hall. The firelight lights the near half of her face in warm orange and leaves the other half in cool blue shadow. Early in the shot she feeds a sheet of yellow joss paper into the fire; its edge curls, blackens and catches, and a few sparks lift toward the beams. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward her face throughout the take. Around the middle of the shot, a dry rustle of paper sounds somewhere off-screen to the right. Her hand, already holding the next sheet of yellow paper, stops halfway to the fire. Her eyes move first, sliding toward the sound; only a beat later does her head turn a fraction in the same direction. Nothing moves in the shadows beyond the paper horses. Her shoulders lower slowly as she breathes out, but her head stays turned slightly toward the dark, and the yellow sheet is still pinched between her fingers. Toward the end, the camera settles on a close shot of her face in the firelight, her eyes still on the darkness, while the flames keep moving and the rain keeps falling beyond the doorway.

Kodak Portra 400 film emulation, about 50% teal-orange grade: teal-leaning shadows, warm amber skin. Fine film grain, soft-focus diffusion, a gentle haze in the air, soft halation on highlights, edges of the frame evenly lit. Lightly handheld throughout: a real handheld micro-shake and breathing motion. Framing stays on the people: chest- or waist-up, with headroom, hands and the key props visible in the lower frame. Real skin texture, visible pores. Only Shen Niangzi, a woman in her thirties, occupies the foreground. Her black hair is gathered into a low bun with a wooden hairpin, and she wears a plain pale grey-green linen jacket and a grey-blue long skirt. She crouches to the left of the brazier, holding yellow paper in her right hand above the near rim. Her left hand rests on her knee. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed from a medium view toward her face and the fire, keeping the raised hand visible near the lower edge. She brings the sheet toward the flames. A dry paper rustle suddenly sounds off-screen to the right. Her hand stops before the sheet enters the fire, the paper still pinched between her fingers. ## overall_soundscape

**官方：** The constant, rhythmic patter of heavy rain against the wooden shop exterior provides a base layer of ambient sound, punctuated by the sharp, crackling hiss of the burning yellow paper as it catches in the brazier. A crisp, brittle rustle of paper occurs from the dark corner, followed by the faint, rhythmic creaking of old floorboards under the woman's weight shift, and the soft, labored breathing of the woman (S1) as she reacts to the noise.

**skill：** Steady rain falls beyond the open doorway and the brazier crackles softly as paper catches and burns. Midway, a single dry rustle of paper sounds off to the right, followed by her slow, quiet breath out, and then only the rain and the fire.

**codex：** Rain outside the doorway and low fire crackle continue beneath a sudden dry rustle of paper off-screen. A small released breath follows, leaving the room quiet around the brazier.
