# tea_pour（8 秒，实际 8.000 秒）

## 简报

写实电影感特写，傍晚茶室，一位老人双手提起紫砂壶往白瓷小杯里倒茶，茶水细细一线落进杯里，满到七分，他把壶放回茶盘，用两根手指把杯子推到对面。暖色侧光，热气升起。没有台词。

## Shot 1

**官方：**

[Shot 1] Cinematic, close-up shot with a slow camera push in, capturing a carved bamboo tea tray on a wooden table in an evening teahouse. Warm amber side lighting streams in from the left, casting deep, textured shadows across the table and illuminating the space. An elderly man, visible only by his dark brown linen tunic and deeply wrinkled, weathered hands, reaches into the frame. With both hands, he carefully lifts a dark purple clay teapot. He tilts the vessel, pouring a thin, steady, continuous line of amber tea into a small, pristine white porcelain cup resting on the tray. Thick white steam billows up from the hot liquid, glowing as it catches the warm side light. Once the tea fills exactly to seventy percent of the small cup's capacity, the man stops pouring and gently lowers the teapot, resting it firmly back onto the slatted wooden tea tray. He then extends his right hand, lightly pressing his index and middle fingers against the outside rim of the white cup, and smoothly pushes it forward across the wooden surface toward the opposite side of the frame.

A small white porcelain cup sits empty on the tray. He lifts a purple-clay teapot with his right hand on the handle and two fingers of his left hand resting on the lid. A soft, hollow thud is clearly heard as the heavy teapot is placed back onto the wooden tray, ending with the pronounced, smooth sliding friction of the porcelain cup being pushed across the wooden table.
